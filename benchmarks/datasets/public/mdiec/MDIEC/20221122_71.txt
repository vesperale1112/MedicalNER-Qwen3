Increased propensity to drink in negative emotional situations was associated with comorbid MDD (beta = 6.6, P = 0.013) and AnxD (beta = 4.8, P = 0.042) as well as a SID x sex interaction effect (P = 0.003), indicating that the association of SID with propensity to drink in negative emotional situations differs by sex and is stronger in males (beta = 7.9, P = 0.009) compared with females (beta = -6.6, P = 0.091).
