Patients with refractory angina had significantly more symptoms of daytime sleepiness (Epworth Sleepiness Scale score, 12 +- 6 vs 8 +- 5; P < .001), had higher depression symptom scores (Beck Depression Inventory score, 19 +- 8 vs 10 +- 8; P < .001) despite greater use of antidepressants, had a higher apnea-hypopnea index (AHI) (AHI, 37 +- 30 events/h vs 23 +- 20 events/h; P = .001), higher proportion of oxygen saturation < 90% during sleep (8% +- 13 vs 4% +- 9, P = .04), and a higher proportion of severe OSA (AHI >= 30 events/h, 48% vs 27%; P = .009) than patients with stable CAD.
