Obesity (odds ratio [OR] 2.3, 95% confidence interval [CI] 1.3-4.0), current smoking (OR 2.7, 95% CI 1.5-4.9), lower educational attainment (OR 2.0, 95% CI 1.2-3.3), moderate incontinence (OR 2.7, 95% CI 1.1-6.6), and severe incontinence (OR 3.8, 95% CI 1.6-9.1) were each associated with increased odds of major depression in women with urinary incontinence, controlling for age and medical comorbidity.
