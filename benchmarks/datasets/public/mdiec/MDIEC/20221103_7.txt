The evidence base of numerous recent clinical studies is discussed as related to their antidepressant efficacy and favorable safety profile, with consideration of multiple agents that target inflammatory mechanisms linked to depression including nonsteroidal anti-inflammatory pathways (i.e., aspirin, celecoxib), cytokine antagonism (i.e., etanercept, infliximab), N-methyl-D-aspartate receptor (NMDA) receptor antagonism (i.e., ketamine), and modulation of kynurenine pathways (i.e., minocycline).
